Grantly: see every rupee you qualify for, and the day each one closes

Grantly is a scholarship discovery and management platform for students. It finds the scholarships you are eligible for, explains why in plain language, and keeps deadlines, documents and applications in one place.

The problem

Finding relevant scholarships, understanding eligibility, navigating application processes and keeping track of deadlines and documents is complicated. Grantly turns it into one simple journey.

Features

  • Scholarship discovery: search, filter by type, amount and deadline, and sort by best match, closing date or award amount.
  • Eligibility matching: checks CGPA, field of study, level, family income, category, state, gender and special status, and shows a reason for every match and every rejection.
  • Personalised dashboard: total aid within reach, ranked matches, and a 60-day funding runway with every deadline on one timeline.
  • Guided onboarding: three short steps, each field explains why it is asked, with live matches as you answer.
  • Application process: step-by-step instructions, document checklist, and a what-if panel showing what it would take to qualify.
  • Application tracker: Saved, Preparing, Submitted and Awarded board with drag and drop and keyboard controls.
  • Document vault: upload once and reuse across applications, with expiry reminders and missing-document alerts.
  • Deadline reminders: export deadlines to your calendar (.ics) and see notifications for anything closing in 10 days.
  • Accessible and responsive: keyboard navigation, screen reader labels, and layouts from 360px phones to desktop.
